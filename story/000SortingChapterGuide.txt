

🧭 **Granular Chapter ID Ranges**

Each range is assigned a **narrative phase, region, theme, or emotional arc**. You can adjust the names to fit your world, but this should give you a solid template to structure and sort your story.

🟢 `000–099` – **Prologue & Setup**

> Player awakening, introduction, early exposition.

* `000–009`: System / Tutorial messages (e.g., `001_intro_ui`)
* `010–019`: Awakening / First memories
* `020–029`: Leaving the safe zone
* `030–039`: First decisions & branching
* `040–049`: Flashbacks / internal conflict
* `050–059`: First encounter (combat / danger)
* `060–099`: Misc. prelude content or lore drops



`100–199` – **Village Life & Key NPCs**

> Home base, social structure, gathering allies.

`100–109`: Village overview (exploration)
`110–119`: Tavern & rumors
`120–129`: Chapel / spiritual insights
`130–139`: Market & trade
`140–149`: Town hall / authority
`150–159`: Village outskirts (small quests)
`160–169`: Personal character interactions
`170–199`: Optional or branching village quests



`200–299` – **Wyrmwood Forest / Wilds**

> Exploration, mystery, danger escalation.

* `200–209`: Entry into the woods
* `210–219`: Environmental puzzles / navigation
* `220–229`: Creature encounters / ambushes
* `230–239`: Spirits, sylphs, and magical phenomena
* `240–249`: Temporary ally or enemy encounters
* `250–259`: Lore nodes / memory echoes
* `260–269`: Sub-bosses or guardian fights
* `270–299`: Major choice point or dungeon entrance


`300–399` – **Ruins, Deep Zones, Lost Places**

> Forgotten knowledge, ancient secrets, descent.

* `300–319`: Cave entry / echo ruins
* `320–339`: Puzzle temples / vaults
* `340–359`: Dreamlike events or reality distortion
* `360–379`: Lost civilizations
* `380–399`: Major reveal or betrayal



`400–499` – **Enemies, Betrayals, and Inner War**

> Heavy narrative turns, mental and emotional strain.

* `400–419`: Captured / separated sequences
* `420–439`: Flashback pain / hallucinations
* `440–459`: Enemy motivations revealed
* `460–479`: Reflections, regrets
* `480–499`: Climax of emotional arc or internal war



`500–599` – **Midpoint: Choice, Transformation, Rebuild**

> Turning point or moment of clarity/change.

* `500–519`: Spiritual/mentor interactions
* `520–539`: Training, regrouping, or upgrade path
* `540–559`: Major moral decision
* `560–579`: Split path confirmation
* `580–599`: End of Act II / re-emergence



`600–699` – **Final Descent / Consequences**

> World's edges, big truths, fatal consequences.

* `600–619`: Night journeys / surrealism
* `620–639`: Forbidden knowledge or choices
* `640–659`: Ally deaths or departures
* `660–679`: Transformation or corruption
* `680–699`: Beginning of endgame



`700–799` – **Endgame / Climax**

> All paths converge toward conclusion.

* `700–729`: Climax setup / rallying forces
* `730–749`: Final trials / gauntlets
* `750–769`: Villain confrontation buildup
* `770–789`: Major deaths / sacrifices
* `790–799`: Final confrontation



### 🕊️ `800–899` – **Post-Final: Epilogue Paths**

> Aftermaths, epilogues, resolution or ruin.

* `800–819`: Peaceful resolution
* `820–839`: Bittersweet endings
* `840–859`: Tragic or dark endings
* `860–879`: Secret or alternate endings
* `880–899`: Timeline resets or paradoxes



`900–999` – **Game Over / Meta / Death Realms**

> Fail states, alternate realities, breaking the fourth wall.

* `900–909`: Player death reactions
* `910–929`: Death realm exploration
* `930–949`: Meta-narrative or memory loops
* `950–969`: Lost souls or failed quests
* `970–999`: Hard resets, rebirths, hidden paths



## 🧠 Tips for Use

* Keep a simple chapter index spreadsheet to track IDs and outputs.
* Use Godot’s `Dictionary.keys().sort()` or a `re.match(r'(\d+)_', id)` method to sort these logically.
* You can **group JSON files** by arc too (e.g., `forest.json`, `village.json`) while keeping unique IDs.
